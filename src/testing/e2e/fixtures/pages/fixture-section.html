§1. On the Osmotic Pressure to be Ascribed to Suspended Particles

Let there be suspended in a liquid a number of small particles.

We shall investigate what state of dynamic equilibrium will be established when a thermodynamic force acts upon each particle. The suspended particles experience a drag determined by the fluid 1.

D = RT 6πkηa D = \frac{RT}{6\pi k \eta a}

This is the fundamental relation connecting osmotic pressure to molecular dimensions.

Viscosity (η)

Measure of a fluid's resistance to gradual deformation by shear or tensile stress.
