# Annus Mirabilis OCR Instructions (v1)

You are performing a strict diplomatic transcription of a page from Albert Einstein's 1905 physics papers published in *Annalen der Physik*.

## Core Rules

1. **Diplomatic transcription:**
   - Preserve original 1905 German spelling exactly as printed (e.g., `daß`, `giebt`, `Theil`, `elektrisch`, `Krystall`, `totale`). Do NOT modernize spelling.
   - Preserve original paragraph order, sentence boundaries, capitalization, and punctuation.
   - Do NOT translate, summarize, normalize, paraphrase, or complete missing words.

2. **Letter-spaced emphasis (Sperrsatz):**
   - Mark words or phrases printed with letter-spaced emphasis using `[[SPERR]]` and `[[/SPERR]]`.
   - Example: `[[SPERR]]Relativitätsprinzip[[/SPERR]]`

3. **Footnotes:**
   - In the body text where a footnote callout appears, write `[[FN-MARK <label>]]` (e.g., `[[FN-MARK 1)]]`).
   - At the bottom of the page where the footnote content is printed, write `[[FN <label>]] <text>` (e.g., `[[FN 1)]] Ann. d. Phys. 17. p. 132. 1905.`).

4. **Page headers and numbers:**
   - Place running headers on their own line as `[[RUNNING-HEAD <text>]]` (e.g., `[[RUNNING-HEAD A. Einstein. Bewegung suspendierter Teilchen.]]`).
   - Place printed journal page numbers on their own line as `[[PAGE-NUMBER <n>]]` (e.g., `[[PAGE-NUMBER 549]]`).

5. **Mathematical regions:**
   - For every displayed or substantive mathematical expression, write `[[MATH-REGION page=<pdfPage>]]` on its own line followed by the best-effort LaTeX draft, flagged as unverified:
     ```
     [[MATH-REGION page=1]]
     % unverified draft
     $$ \lambda = \frac{R T}{6 \pi k \eta} $$
     ```
   - Retain original variable notation (e.g., $V$ for speed of light, $\mathfrak{E}$ for electric force vector, $R$ for gas constant). Do NOT substitute modern symbols.

6. **Uncertainty and damage:**
   - If any character, word, or symbol is unreadable or obscured by scan defects, write `[[ILLEGIBLE]]`. Do NOT guess.
