## Notation difficulties

Dangerous collisions to flag on first use, still without unit ids because source units are absent:

- $k$ is viscosity, not Boltzmann's constant.
- $P$ is particle radius, not pressure.
- $\nu$ is number density, not frequency.
- $N$ is Avogadro's number; $R$ is the gas constant and is not expected as a printed numeral.
- $\varphi(\Delta)$ is the transition kernel; $\tau$ is an observation interval.
- $K$ is a force. Printed unit words expected in §5: *Mikron*, *Sek.*
