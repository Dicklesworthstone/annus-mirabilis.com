## 1. Bibliographic Identities

### A. Doctoral Dissertation (1906 Publication)
- **Author**: Albert Einstein
- **Title (German)**: *Eine neue Bestimmung der Moleküldimensionen*
- **Journal**: *Annalen der Physik* (4. Folge), Band 19, Heft 2, Seiten 289–306 (1906)
- **Whole-Series Volume**: Band 324, Heft 2
- **Canonical DOI**: `10.1002/andp.19063240205`
- **Academic Context**: Completed April 30, 1905; submitted to the University of Zurich on July 20, 1905; approved July 24, 1905; published in *Annalen* January 1906.
- **Collected Papers**: *The Collected Papers of Albert Einstein*, Vol. 2, Doc. 15.

### B. The 1911 Correction
- **Author**: Albert Einstein
- **Title (German)**: *Berichtigung zu meiner Arbeit: „Eine neue Bestimmung der Moleküldimensionen“*
- **Journal**: *Annalen der Physik* (4. Folge), Band 34, Heft 3, Seiten 591–592 (1911)
- **Whole-Series Volume**: Band 339, Heft 3
- **Canonical DOI**: `10.1002/andp.19113390313`
- **Correction Significance**: Corrects the hydrodynamic coefficient of volume fraction $\varphi$ in the suspension viscosity formula from 1 to $5/2$ (2.5), following Ludwig Hopf's check, bringing the deduced Avogadro constant into close agreement with Perrin's results.
- **Collected Papers**: *The Collected Papers of Albert Einstein*, Vol. 3, Doc. 14.
