### Calibration of the embedded layer, for whoever transcribes next

Measured over pages 132–137 rather than assumed, because it tells you which class of layer output
to trust and which to retype:

- **It reads German inflections BETTER than a careful reader does.** It corrected three of my
  readings on this paper, every one a small grammatical form: `Punkten` not `Punkte`,
  `im Anschluß` not `in Anschluß`, and on ap-17-549 `seien` not `sind` — the last changing a
  sentence from an assertion to a statement of an assumption's content. Where the layer and a
  reading disagree on an inflection, check the layer first.

  `L` as `Z`, the force `K` as `X` where both letters appear in one sentence, `μ` as `u`, and a

## Suspected historical typographical errors

Six. Five were found while transcribing and read from the pinned page images at 400 dpi or
finer; the sixth, `typo-x-for-alpha-p135`, was found on 2026-09-24 while correcting the ledger from
the plates, read at 300 and 600 dpi. Full records under `typographicalErrors` in the front matter. **The ledger keeps every original
reading**, as a diplomatic transcription must; nothing below has been corrected in the source
face, and no comparison witness was consulted for any of them.

Five of the six are settled by the page contradicting itself or by dimensions, which is the
kind a reviewer can re-check without re-deriving the conclusion.

- **`typo-x-for-alpha-p135`**: in the footnote that defines radiation as *denkbar ungeordnet*
  (§1, marked 2) on the page), the sentence restating the product condition reads `einer der
  Größen A bez. α … welche die anderen Größen A bez. x besitzen`. The footnote defines no x, and
  the product it restates runs over the A and the α only. The ledger keeps the x; the explanation
  passage `arg-lq-equilibrium-assumptions` names the misprint in its margin.
- **`typo-dropped-T-footnote-p142`** — the footnote prints `T dS = R (n/N) (dv/v)`, equating an
  energy to an entropy. The body gives `dS = R (n/N) (dv/v)`, and the footnote's own next line,
  `p v = R (n/N) T`, follows only if the right-hand side carries a factor `T`.
- **`typo-nachdem-p142`** — `nachdem` set as one word where the sense requires `nach dem`
  referring back to *das Gesetz*: "the law **according to which** the molecules move".
- **`typo-bezeichnung-p144`** — `es muß also die Bezeichnung gelten:` introducing the Stokes
  inequality, where the sense requires `Beziehung`. An inequality is a relation; a designation
  cannot hold of two energies. The word breaks `Be-` / `zeichnung` across the line.
- **`typo-pi-exponent-p146`** — the page states `Π · 10⁻⁸ das Potential in Volts` and four lines
  later reports `Π · 10⁷ = 4,3 Volt`. Both cannot hold; they differ by fifteen orders of
  magnitude. The minus is plainly set in the first and plainly absent in the second.
- **`typo-pe-for-pi-p147`** — `ist P E stets bedeutend größer als R β ν`, where the sentence's own
  justification (`da die Spannung …`) names Π, and where `P E` is an energy times a charge and so
  is not comparable with `R β ν` at all.

**For whoever builds the photoelectric instrument:** use the printed `4,3 Volt` and the `10⁻⁸`
conversion, never the `10⁷` factor. The volts are a sound historical fixture; the factor is not.
