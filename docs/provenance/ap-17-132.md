### Calibration of the embedded layer, for whoever transcribes next

Measured over pages 132–137 rather than assumed, because it tells you which class of layer output
to trust and which to retype:

- **It reads German inflections BETTER than a careful reader does.** It corrected three of my
  readings on this paper, every one a small grammatical form: `Punkten` not `Punkte`,
  `im Anschluß` not `in Anschluß`, and on ap-17-549 `seien` not `sind` — the last changing a
  sentence from an assertion to a statement of an assumption's content. Where the layer and a
  reading disagree on an inflection, check the layer first.
- **It reads mathematical glyphs far worse, and fails silently.** It renders the speed of light
  `L` as `Z`, the force `K` as `X` where both letters appear in one sentence, `μ` as `u`, and a
  mean-energy overbar as `Z` on one line and a bare `E` two lines later. None of these announces
  itself as an error.
- **It DROPS display equations entirely rather than garbling them.** On page 136 the α and β
  lines are absent from every extraction mode — `-layout`, `-raw` and the default all return
  nothing for them. An absent line offers no second reading at all, which is why
  `watch-alpha-exponent` is recorded as a single-witness plate reading rather than confirmed.
- **Practical rule:** trust it on word forms, never on a symbol or a number, and treat the
  absence of a display line as evidence of nothing rather than as evidence the line is empty.
