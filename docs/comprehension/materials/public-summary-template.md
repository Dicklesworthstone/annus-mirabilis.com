## Our Approach to Testing Understanding

We believe that high-quality scientific explanations must be evaluated with real readers. We do not assume that an explanation is clear because it seems obvious to its authors.

To test the clarity and accessibility of the 1905 papers, we conduct structured, compensated comprehension rounds across four distinct routes:
1. **No-Algebra Route (`no-algebra`):** Readers exploring conceptual bridges, visual models, and intuitive step-by-step prose without mathematical prerequisites.
2. **Nonvisual Route (`nonvisual`):** Blind and low-vision readers using screen readers, keyboard navigation, and auditory descriptions.
3. **Full-Derivation Route (`full-derivation`):** Readers examining step-by-step mathematical chains, notation concordances, and historical derivations.
4. **Mobile Route (`low-cost-phone`):** Readers accessing the edition on entry-level smartphones over mobile network connections.

## Empirical Boundary & Humble Scope

We make no universal claim that this website will make advanced theoretical physics effortless for every person. Different people bring different perspectives, backgrounds, and goals.

Our empirical claim is modest and precise: *for these specific arguments, the readers in our testing rounds were able to navigate the explanations, understand the core reasoning, and successfully apply the ideas to new transfer problems.* We continuously test, fix identified stumbling points, and publish our updated findings here.
