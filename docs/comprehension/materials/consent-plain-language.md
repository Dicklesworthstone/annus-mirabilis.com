# Participant Information & Consent Form (Plain Language)

**Study Title:** Annus Mirabilis Interactive Reader Comprehension Study
**Institution / Project:** Annus Mirabilis Critical Edition (`annus-mirabilis.com`)

---

## 1. What is this study about?
We are testing new interactive explanations and historical editions of Albert Einstein's 1905 physics papers. We want to understand where our explanations, diagrams, and interactive tools are clear, and where they create obstacles or confusion.

**Important:** We are testing our website, NOT you. There are no right or wrong answers. Any difficulty you experience is valuable feedback that helps us improve the site.

---

## 2. What will you do?
- You will explore a lesson, interactive diagram, or paper translation on a computer or phone.
- You will be asked to "think aloud"—saying what you notice, what makes sense, and what is confusing.
- The session takes approximately 30 to 45 minutes.
- You may pause, take a break, or stop completely at any time.

---

## 3. Voluntary Participation & Right to Stop
Participation is entirely voluntary. You have the right to stop the session at any moment without providing any reason. Stopping early will not affect your compensation or your relationship with the project team.

---

## 4. Privacy & Data Minimization
- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or demographic scores in the public research records.
- You are identified in all published notes only by a pseudonymous participant code (e.g. `brownian-motion-nonvisual-20270412-03`).
- Notes and anonymous interaction logs are retained for 12 months for research verification, after which raw private records are securely deleted.
- If audio or screen recording is requested, it requires your separate, explicit permission and is never shared publicly.

---

## 5. Compensation & Accommodations
- You will receive compensation for your time as agreed prior to the session.
- We welcome assistive technologies (screen readers, screen magnifiers, switch access) and provide flexible scheduling.
