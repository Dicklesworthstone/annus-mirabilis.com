Annus Mirabilis Reader Comprehension Study

Accessible Participant Information and Informed Consent

1. Study Purpose & Non-Judgmental Stance

This research study evaluates the accessibility and clarity of the interactive scientific edition Annus Mirabilis. We test whether our interactive diagrams, nonvisual representations, and explanations are effective.

Important notice: You are not being tested or graded. Our software and explanations are being tested. Any difficulty or confusion you encounter informs our improvements.

2. Session Procedure

  • You will review an interactive lesson or translation using your preferred screen reader, keyboard, or switch device.
  • You will be invited to speak your thoughts aloud as you navigate.
  • Sessions take approximately 30 to 45 minutes.
  • You may pause or stop at any moment.

3. Privacy, Pseudonymity & Data Minimization

No personal contact information or medical details are recorded. All notes use a pseudonymous code (such as brownian-motion-nonvisual-20270412-03). Anonymous records are retained for 12 months for research verification.

4. Voluntary Participation & Compensation

Participation is completely voluntary. You receive agreed compensation regardless of whether you finish all tasks or choose to stop early.

5. Consent Declaration

By providing verbal or written agreement to the facilitator, you confirm that:

  1. You have reviewed this accessible information.
  2. You voluntarily agree to participate.
  3. You know you may stop at any time without penalty.
